Utkarsh Giri

Postdoctoral Fellow
California Institute of Technology

I am a cosmologist working on statistical methods for extracting information from large-scale astrophysical datasets. At Caltech, I work on data analysis and inference pipelines for the SPHEREx satellite mission.

My research brings together cosmological surveys, N-body simulations, and statistical inference, with interests in primordial non-Gaussianity, cosmic velocity reconstruction, and machine learning.

Previously, I was a research associate at the University of Wisconsin–Madison. I received my Ph.D. in Physics from the University of Waterloo and Perimeter Institute, advised by Kendrick M. Smith.

Research

I develop methods for learning about cosmology from the statistical structure of survey data. My work includes neural network-enhanced estimators of primordial non-Gaussianity, kinematic Sunyaev–Zeldovich velocity reconstruction, and Bayesian analysis of fast radio bursts as part of the CHIME/FRB Collaboration.

I am particularly interested in connecting theoretical models, numerical simulations, and inference pipelines. I also write open-source research software and explore data science and machine learning through side projects.
